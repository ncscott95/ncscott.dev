This week, I spent basically all of my time working on player animations. While I feel I am starting to make progress, it has been quite the struggle to find and maintain momentum. 


### Final Character Concept

At the start of this week, I finally created the first final sprites for my character’s design. The biggest struggle was striking a balance between making the design representative of my inspirations (Chinese martial arts and wuxia fashion), while also being feasible to draw with my beginner skills. 

For instance, while I had mentioned a glowing arm tattoo at the end of last week’s devlog, I quickly realized that making it consistent and recognizable would simply take too much effort away from the rest of my art. Instead, I settled on a simple robe with plain strips of material trailing down from the front and back. Hopefully, this will evoke the flowing fabrics of typical wuxia clothing. 


### Animation Progress

Finally, with a concrete character design, I have started creating animation assets for the main character. So far, I have completed the basic rope dart spinning loop, and am almost done with the lead arm elbow shot! 

<img src="/images/2026-08-23-elbow-shot.gif">

While progress has been slow so far, I can definitely feel my intuition for pixel art improving as I work. Hopefully, I can keep growing this momentum and gradually accelerate my output throughout the next week. For a minimum viable product (MVP), I will need about 30 total animations. However, about half of those are basically mirrored versions of other animations, albeit with adjustments for the lead arm still facing the camera. 


### Sprint 5 Statistics

This sprint marked a bit of a step backwards in terms of overall productivity and consistency, as I only worked about 38 hours compared to Sprint 4’s 60 hours. I think this is mainly due to my overall slow progress with learning pixel art, which makes maintaining motivation pretty difficult. However, as mentioned above, I’m cautiously optimistic that I can start to overcome this as my confidence grows.

### Coming Up

As of now, I only have four more weeks of development before reaching my deadline of September 20. While I expect to spend a few days on emergency fixes after the deadline, my goal is to have a fully playable MVP by that date. This means I really need to be done with player animations by the end of this next two-week sprint. As such, next week will be almost solely dedicated to art and animation!
