I have returned from vacation! Not much work was completed this last week so this will be a shorter update, but I’m looking forward to getting back to the grind. 


### Development Timeline

Since I am now halfway through the second of my seven sprints, it’s time to set some serious deadlines to keep myself on track. At the beginning of the project, I outlined these initial milestones: 

* Sprint 1: Final Game Concept
* Sprint 2: First Prototype
* Sprint 3: Closed Playtest
* Sprint 4: Open Playtest

I will likely need to reframe the sprint 3 and 4 goals for multiple reasons, not least of which being that I completely forgot to account for the two weeks I just spent on vacation. I also planned these around a game of much smaller scope, and the closed playtest was supposed to include all features in the game. 


### Updated Timeline

Since my rope dart combat system is complex and will require a decent amount of art and animation to be playable, I am reframing the sprint 3 milestone to be a demo of just the combat engine. Sprint 4 will then be focused on refining the combat engine based on feedback, then starting to flesh out level design and other features. If everything goes well, sprint 4 will be the new closed playtest. 

With the game’s new scope and my current progress in mind, here are my new sprint milestones:

- Sprint 1: Final Game Concept (Success)
- Sprint 2: First Prototype
- Sprint 3: Playable Combat Demo
- Sprint 4: Closed Playtest


### Coming Up

For the rest of this week, I will be working almost solely on the rope dart combat mechanics. By Monday, I will ideally be in a position where I can start creating simple sprites and animations to make the prototype more visually intuitive. Additionally, now that I’m back to a normal work schedule, I’ll hopefully be posting these devlogs consistently every Sunday. 

That’s all for this week, thanks for reading!
