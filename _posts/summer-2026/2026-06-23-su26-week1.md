This week, I spent most of my time ideating and creating an initial prototype, and I am happy to announce that I have committed to the game I will be making! 


### The Idea

I will be making a 2D sidescrolling beat-em-up centered around precision combat using the traditional Chinese [rope dart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rope_dart){:target="_blank"}. The game will be structured as a series of levels, and the player will receive a score for each level based on kills, damage taken, and "style," loosely similar to Ultrakill. My main philosophy for the combat system is to give the player as much freedom and control as possible and allow them to perform moves and sequences that actually exist. As such, combat will have a somewhat steep learning curve, but will hopefully be satisfying to master. 

I am pulling from a number of inspirations for this project. First and foremost, I have wanted to make a custom combat system for a long time now, as souls-likes and metroidvanias have always been my favorite game genres. For the rope dart itself, I was recently inspired by content creator [Instructor Bensei](https://www.youtube.com/@instructor_bensei){:target="_blank"}. I am still workshopping ideas for a setting and theme, and I will hopefully have those more fleshed out by the end of this next week. 


### Initial Prototype

Since this idea centers around a complex, precise combat system, I created a proof-of-concept to make sure that this is reasonable for my 14-week timeline. In the later half of this week, I implemented: 

- a basic 2D character controller
- preliminary rope dart mechanics
  - spinning the rope
  - stalling spins to switch direction
  - casting the dart out and retrieving it
  - twining (wrapping) the rope around an elbow point
- a reusable input buffering interface

I feel pretty happy with this prototype, and while this game is shaping up to be relatively high-scope, I am confident and passionate enough about the idea to commit to it long-term.
