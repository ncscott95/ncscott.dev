## VGDC

The Video Game Development Club (VGDC) @ UC San Diego is a student organization dedicated to helping students achieve success in the game development industry. It is also the community in which I do almost of my development projects! I first joined the club as a member in Fall 2024 (my first quarter in college), before being accepted as a Programming Lead officer in Spring 2025, a role I have held ever since. 



## The Chanel Project

Every year starting in 2022, VGDC runs The Chanel Project (TCP), a massive quarter-long (or longer) undertaking that every member of the club can join! Members are free to pitch their dream game proposals and everyone votes for the final idea. Every year, over one hundred people have signed up and contributed to these amazing projects! 

As a programming lead for VGDC, I am responsible for managing the largest of TCP's teams. Programming team is typically divided into feature-based subteams, each of which has an appointed subteam lead. I work closely with design team and the other discipline leads to write up programming tasks, which are in turn assigned to members by these subteam leads. I also manage the GitHub repository, review pull requests, and help pick up miscellaneous tasks.
