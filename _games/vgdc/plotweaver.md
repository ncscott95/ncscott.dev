*Plotweaver* is a roguelike deckbuilding card game where every card contributes to an ever-growing story. This was my personal Flare pitch, and I led an 18-person team for the duration of the quarter. While it isn't the most polished, this was my first experience holding a leadership position for an game's full development, and I learned a lot of lessons that I carry into all of my future projects.
