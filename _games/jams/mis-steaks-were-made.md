Submitted to the inaugural Triton Game Development Challenge (TGDC) in Fall 2026, *mis-steaks were made.* is a softbody pachinko incremental game where you raise your steaks and bet on them in chaotic races! I primarily worked on procedurally generating these squishy mis-steaks and handling their softbody collisions.
